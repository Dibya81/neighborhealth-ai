# NeighborHealth terms of use

*Draft for the student pilot; ▢ fill in your name and contact, and have it reviewed before a public launch.*

1. **This is information, not medical advice.** NeighborHealth shows dengue-risk estimates and answers questions
   from official guidelines (WHO, Indian Ministry of Health). It cannot tell you whether you have dengue.
   If you have **severe stomach pain, repeated vomiting, bleeding, difficulty breathing, drowsiness or collapse, go to
   a hospital now or call 108.** For anything else about your health, see a doctor or call 104.
2. **Estimates can be wrong.** Forecasts come from reported cases for the whole BBMP area. Ward-level numbers are
   *model estimates*, not counted cases, and always come with a range. ▢ State the date of the latest data being
   used on the home page; do not describe a forecast as current if its data is old.
3. **Not an emergency service.** Alerts and reports are not monitored around the clock.
4. **Reports.** Only report real mosquito-breeding spots. Do not include people, faces, number plates or private
   property you have no right to photograph. Fake or abusive reports may be removed and accounts closed.
   You keep ownership of your photos and allow us to use them to verify the report and, without your name,
   to show hotspots.
5. **Your account.** Keep your password private. You can delete your account at any time (see the privacy notice).
6. **Alerts.** You can stop them at any time. We try not to send more than two a week per person.
7. **No guarantee.** The service is provided as it is, may be unavailable, and is run by volunteers.
   To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for decisions made on the basis of its estimates.
8. **Changes.** If we change these terms or what we collect, we will tell you in the app and ask again
   where the law requires.
